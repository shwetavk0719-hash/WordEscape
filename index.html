GAME GUIDE

How to Play

Your mission is to solve the word puzzles and escape the laboratory.

1

Read the clue

Every room gives you a clue. Think of the word that matches it.

2

Type the answer

Enter your answer in the box and press Unlock.

3

Use hints

If you are stuck, use a hint. A hint reveals one letter but costs points.

4

Escape!

Solve all the puzzles before losing all your lives or running out of time.
